¶ Colophon

1973 is an independent agency. It makes small, useful things on the open web — directories, tools, chatbots, books, music — built quickly and shipped without ceremony.

The name is a year, not a brand strategy. It marks a sensibility: late enough to be raised on the internet, early enough to remember when it wasn't there. The team works from across the world, with senior hands on every project. That tension is the work.

Most of what 1973 makes is built for itself — products, tools, experiments released to the open web. Some work is for clients, by invitation. The line between the two isn't always clean, and that suits us.
